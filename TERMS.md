## 1. What Aetherphone is

Aetherphone is a free, open-source plugin for Dalamud, a community plugin framework for FINAL FANTASY XIV. It draws a smartphone inside the game. Some of its apps work entirely on your computer. Others connect to Aethernet, a hosted service that provides accounts, profiles, feeds, messaging, and calls between Aetherphone users. The Aetherphone companion app for iOS and Android signs in to the same Aethernet account and offers some of the social apps outside the game.

Aetherphone is provided free of charge. No app or feature requires payment. Voluntary support is described in section 10.

## 11. Moderation, suspension, and termination

Moderation on Aethernet is done by people. Content is not screened automatically, and no AI moderates it. Moderators act on reports and can also review content directly, including, for some moderators, conversations that are not end-to-end encrypted, as described in the Privacy Policy. Moderators do not review every post. You can report posts, profiles, messages, musters, and Yellow Pages ads from inside the app.

When content breaks these terms or an app's conduct rules, a moderator may:

- Cover it with a veil, so that viewers see it blurred until they choose to look
- Remove it, which sends you a warning naming the rule it broke
- Remove it and suspend your account

Moderators can also reset a display name or handle, and remove profile images. Repeated violations can lead to a suspension. The most serious rules, including the child safety rules, call for a suspension on the first violation.

A suspension signs you out and locks the social apps for its duration. It does not delete your content. While it lasts, the suspended character cannot sign in or create a new account, and your Aether Coin stays frozen until 7 days after it ends. The moderator sets the length within the limit of their role, and a suspension can be permanent. A permanent suspension terminates the account's access to Aethernet. We may act without prior notice where we consider it necessary to enforce these terms or to protect users.

When content of yours is removed or veiled, or your account is suspended, the phone shows you a notice that explains the action, with the rule involved and any note the moderator added. You can appeal by opening a support ticket on our [Discord](https://discord.gg/3HbJCscMyS) server or by writing to the address in section 15.

Reports and records of moderation actions have no automatic deletion date. The Privacy Policy sets out what is kept.
